# foraging_sb

A deterministic, seeded port of the **foraging** task of SwarmBench for the Antelligence v4 pilot.

* SwarmBench: <https://github.com/x66ccff/swarmbench> (MIT licence), paper <https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.04364>.
  The layouts, the 2x2 food and nest blocks, the scoring (deliveries) and the evaluation settings (10 agents,
  100 rounds, 10x10 map, 5x5 view) come from there. SwarmBench's code was read, never run or imported.
* Everything else (push rule, sensing, scripts, variants) is new code in this package.

## What the world is

Ten agents on a 10x10 walled map. A 2x2 food patch and a 2x2 nest sit on opposite sides of one serpentine wall
with a gap at one end (four published layouts: wall horizontal or vertical, food at either end). An agent
carries one piece at a time: stand next to the food and `pick_up`, stand next to the nest and `drop` to score
one delivery. Score = deliveries after 100 rounds.

Each agent sees an egocentric 5x5 window (`W` wall, `F` food, `N` nest, `*` outside the map, a digit for another
agent, `Y` itself) and knows its own position. Peers talk through the world, never in free text:
after each tick every agent that can see the nest (when it carries food) or the food (otherwise) broadcasts
`"<food|nest> is <compass direction> of me"` (at most 120 characters). Listeners within 4 cells (Chebyshev) hear it
on the next tick, with the speaker's relative position. The sensor writes the two nearest into the state text.

Conditions (instructions, scorer and action list are identical in all three):

| Condition | What breaks |
|---|---|
| `base` | nothing; food is unlimited |
| `v1` "food runs out" | the patch empties after 6 pickups; a fresh patch appears on the far side of the same lane |
| `v2` "sensor flips" | from tick 30 the west-east axis of every agent's view (and of the directions it hears) is mirrored; the world is not |

## Actions and checks

`north`, `east`, `south`, `west`, `pick_up`, `drop`, `stay` (the scheduler's `noop` is also accepted).
Checks (judgement nouls, at most two per tick): *"Is the food patch you remember still there?"* and *"Is the nest
where you remembered it?"* (offered only once the agent has the corresponding memory).

## Sensing

`ForagingSensor` turns an agent's `LocalView` into a `SensedState`: about 300-400 characters in words ("Carrying food:
no. Remembered food spot: 3 north, 2 east. That spot is in view and food is there. Remembered nest: 4 south. Heard:
agent 7 (1 east of you) says "food is north-west of me". Best known next step toward the food: go east."). Directions
and the route hint are computed in code. The remembered food and nest are what the agent was briefed on, moved only
when it picks up or drops there; seeing the spot empty, or food somewhere else, never changes them. That is what makes
a script go stale under v1 and v2.

## Scripts and validity

* **S**: shortest path to the remembered food, carry to the remembered nest, explore unseen ground when nothing is
  remembered; steps aside when blocked (seeded by the agent's per-tick seed).
* **S' for v1**: S, but forgets a remembered food spot that is in view and empty, and goes to food it sees elsewhere.
* **S' for v2**: S with its own map built from the view, deciding from the walls it already knows whether the view is
  mirrored; with no evidence it learns nothing from that view.

`python -m antelligence.worlds.foraging_sb.variants` reruns the validity check on the development seeds (100..109) and
rewrites `docs/research/v4-pilot-20261011/validity-foraging_sb.json`. No model calls are involved.

## Departures from SwarmBench

1. **Physics.** SwarmBench resolves pushes with an integer-programming/mesh solver. Here: a step into an empty cell
   moves; a step into one or two agents in a row moves all of them if the cell behind is free (agent weight 1, force 2);
   walls, food and nest never move; anything else is refused. Moves resolve one agent at a time in agent-id order,
   not simultaneously.
2. **Pick-up and drop** are explicit actions (`pick_up`, `drop`), not automatic when adjacent.
3. **Action set**: `north/east/south/west` replace `UP/DOWN/LEFT/RIGHT`; `MSG` is gone.
4. **Messages** are generated by the world by a fixed rule, not written by the agent; the speaker id is shown (SwarmBench
   messages are anonymous); radius 4 instead of the view radius 2.
5. **Briefing.** Agents start knowing which cell of the food patch and of the nest to head for (SwarmBench agents start
   with nothing). The wall layout is unknown and discovered from the view. This makes stale memory well defined for v1/v2.
6. **Memory** is a per-agent sticky map plus the two remembered cells, reported by the world; SwarmBench prompts carry the
   last five views and actions instead.
7. **View** does not mark which other agents carry food (SwarmBench prefixes non-carriers with `$`); the agent's own
   position is given as numbers rather than printed axis labels.
8. **Agent ids** are `"0"`..`"9"` (SwarmBench `Agent_0`..). Start cells use the same seeded draws and order as
   SwarmBench's `spread`, assuming agents are placed in id order; this was not checked by running SwarmBench.
9. **Variants v1 and v2, the sensor, the typed actions/checks, S and S'** do not exist in SwarmBench.
10. Only the foraging task is ported (not transport, flocking, pursuit, synchronisation).
